# Indicator words by wordplay type

Indicators tell the solver which operation to perform. In a fair clue the
operation must be signalled by a word that legitimately means that operation.
This is a working palette, not a closed list. Choose one that also pulls its
weight in the surface reading.

## Anagram (rearrange the letters)
Anything meaning disturbed, broken, cooked, moving or wrong:
- broken, smashed, shattered, damaged, ruined, wrecked, bent, twisted
- cooked, baked, stewed, mashed, ground, boiled, roasted
- drunk, wild, mad, crazy, upset, disturbed, troubled, confused, awkward
- new, novel, fresh, strange, odd, curious, unusual, oddly
- arranged, ordered, designed, worked, fashioned, engineered, treated
- dancing, swaying, spinning, whirling, all over the place, at sea, out
- possibly, perhaps, maybe, sort of, could be (weaker; use sparingly)

## Reversal (letters read backwards)
For across clues use turning words; for down clues, upward motion:
- back, returned, reversed, turned, about, retreating, going west (across)
- rising, mounting, climbing, up, uplifted, northward (down clues only)
- recalled, sent back, brought back, in retreat, overturned, upset

## Hidden word (letters sit consecutively inside the fodder)
- in, within, inside, held by, contained in, part of, some, some of
- hidden in, buried in, concealed in, covered by, harbouring
- a little, a bit of, sample of, section of, extract from, cross-section

## Container / insertion (one string wraps or enters another)
- in, into, inside, within, entering, invading, penetrating (X goes in Y)
- around, about, surrounding, holding, embracing, grasping, clutching (Y holds X)
- outside, wrapping, framing, bordering, containing, swallowing, eating

## Deletion (remove letters)
- headless / topless (remove first letter), beheaded, decapitated
- endless / tailless / curtailed / docked / short (remove last letter)
- gutless / heartless (remove middle), edited, cut, clipped, trimmed
- almost, nearly, most of (drop the last letter), unfinished

## Homophone (sounds like)
- we hear, they say, reportedly, on the radio, aloud, audibly, spoken
- sounds, said, announced, vocal, by the sound of it, to the ear, broadcast

## Selection (take specific letters)
- initially / firstly / at first / leaders / heads / tops (first letters)
- finally / at last / ends / tails / endings (last letters)
- centre / heart / middle (middle letters)
- odd / even (odd- or even-positioned letters), regularly, alternately

## Charade (juxtaposition — usually needs no indicator)
Pieces are simply written in order. Link words (with, on, over, after,
before, following, then, and, by, beside) join them and read as surface.
In down clues, "on"/"over"/"above" can imply the second piece sits under the
first, so respect the geometry.

## Link words (definition ↔ wordplay join)
Fair, near-invisible joins: is, are, makes, gives, becomes, from, for, in,
"a" / "an", "'s" (is/has), yields, produces, thus, so, we get, providing.
Avoid links that assert a false equivalence direction; keep them honest.

## &lit / semi-&lit
The whole clue is simultaneously the definition and the wordplay (an "&lit",
marked with a trailing "!"), or the definition overlaps the wordplay
(semi-&lit). No separate definition word: the entire surface must parse both
as literal wordplay and as a definition of the answer.
